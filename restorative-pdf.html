Professional Summary

I am an NVC practitioner and facilitator with 10+ years of embodied practice building collaborative, democratic frameworks inside a worker-owned cooperative. I think of myself as a collaboration specialist: my work centers facilitation, conflict transformation, and accountability processes that prioritize dignity and repair.

I bring this practice into workplaces because teams need it to thrive. In cooperatives and mission-driven organizations especially, I want to serve the work they are doing by helping them do it with less friction and more joy. Better outcomes for workers and for the mission.

I am applying to join the RJI Community of Practice because I believe in community. I want to contribute to and learn from practitioners who love this work as much as I do.
